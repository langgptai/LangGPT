# Math Tutors / 数学私教

Two reusable LangGPT tutoring roles, each available in English and Simplified Chinese.
两套可复用的 LangGPT 数学私教提示词，均提供英文和简体中文版。

Author / 作者: [Gugu-xiu](https://github.com/Gugu-xiu)

| Role / 角色 | English | 简体中文 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calculus / 高等数学 | [Calculus tutor](calculus_tutor_en.md) | [高等数学私教](calculus_tutor_zh.md) |
| Linear algebra / 线性代数 | [Linear algebra tutor](linear_algebra_tutor_en.md) | [线性代数私教](linear_algebra_tutor_zh.md) |

## Capabilities / 功能

- **Calculus:** concept explanations, justified worked solutions, revision notes and useful corollaries, and personalized study plans.
- **高等数学：** 概念讲解、解题思路、二级结论与知识总结、个性化学习建议，保留四个完整技能模块及回复结构。
- **Linear algebra:** explain each calculation through its algebraic operation, purpose, and geometric meaning; visualize transformations; connect vector spaces, span, independence, bases, and dimension.
- **线性代数：** 通过“代数操作＋步骤目的＋几何意义”拆解计算，用几何直觉解释变换，并构建概念关系图谱，保留三个完整技能模块。

## How to use / 使用方式

1. Open one prompt in your preferred language and copy the complete file into a new conversation, or into the assistant instructions if your application supports that.
2. Provide your topic or problem, current level, and main point of confusion. For a study plan, add your goal and available study time.
3. Respond to the tutor's check-in questions so it can adapt the explanation.

1. 打开所需语言的提示词，将完整内容复制到新对话，或放入应用支持的助手指令中。
2. 补充题目、当前基础和困惑点；制定计划时再提供学习目标及可用时间。
3. 回答理解检查问题，让助手调整讲解深度和节奏。

## Example inputs / 输入示例

- **Calculus:** “Explain why the derivative of sin(x) is cos(x), starting from the limit definition. I know basic limits but struggle with the geometric interpretation.”
- **高数：** “我会求简单极限，但不理解导数的几何意义。请从极限定义讲解为什么 sin(x) 的导数是 cos(x)。”
- **Linear algebra:** “Diagonalize A = [[4, -2], [1, 1]]. For each stage, explain the algebra, its purpose, and its geometric meaning.”
- **线代：** “请将 A = [[4, -2], [1, 1]] 对角化。每一步都解释代数操作、步骤目的和几何意义。”

## Adaptation and validation / 整理说明

These prompts were adapted from Chinese tutoring prompts used by the contributor. The English versions use natural English mathematical terminology and preserve the full teaching structures. Both languages add explicit LangGPT profile, goals, workflow, and initialization sections. Formatting was normalized, citation placeholders were clarified, and tool-dependent capabilities and mathematical assumptions were made explicit.

提示词来自贡献者使用过的中文数学私教稿。整理版保留完整教学结构，补充 LangGPT 的角色资料、目标、工作流和初始化部分；规范引用格式，清理不可见字符，并明确工具能力和数学条件。英文版采用自然英语表达，适用于大学数学学习及考试准备。

The reformatted bilingual versions have not yet been independently evaluated across models. Diagrams, file handling, current-source lookup, and reminders depend on the host application's available tools. No generated model responses or benchmark results are claimed here.

整理后的中英文版本尚未完成跨模型独立评测。图像、文件、联网核实和提醒能力取决于所用应用；此处不声称已获得模型输出或基准测试结果。
